## PART 0 — REGISTER SCOPING (train-graded)
**THE PRINCIPLE: A GRADED LAW CARRIES FORCE ONLY INSIDE THE REGISTER THAT GRADED IT.** Every rule below this
section was measured on particular pages of particular kinds. On any other kind of page it is not a weak rule,
it is NO rule — a guess wearing the clothes of evidence. An out-of-register law may ADVISE; it may never
override a law graded on the page in front of me, and it may never override the count. This is not a
preference: importing a law across registers is the largest repeated class of error in my own train record,
and it is the one class that never announces itself, because the law is mine and it is TRUE where it was
measured. Every rule in this file is to be read as if the name of its register were printed inside it.

**S1. THE RULE, AND ITS PROCEDURE. Before applying any law, name the register it was graded on AND the register
in front of me. If they are not the same page type, the law advises and nothing more. Where NO graded law
covers the page in front of me, fall back on the mechanical count — name the canonical unit and count units —
and invent no connective policy.** The proof that the SHAPE decides nothing is one shape read three ways in
three train registers: `,` + و + a finite verb with the SAME subject. PA part 7 (batch D2, PA23) killed it for
children's PA narrative on eight graded false cuts in one document — `وتدفق منها الدم` · `واهتزت نفسه` ·
`وتغير تفكيره فجأة` · `وقرر أن يأذن لابنه`. PA part 11 (batch C3, PA34) killed it a second time for a
children's-magazine biography feature — false cuts at `ولد في روسيا في العام 1844 , ونشأ في أسرة فنية` and
`واظب على الدروس الموسيقية , وتعلم على أيدي كبار الموسيقيين الروس`. And the NP appendix (NP7, al-ʿAqqād's
«سارة») records the OPPOSITE verdict: twenty-two recall errors from MERGING that same shape in ornate literary
narrative — `وسبيل التواريخ أن تنطوي السير` · `واستظرف همام جوابها` · `ورحب مع ذلك بهذه المشاركة`. Three
registers, three verdicts, one shape. NP7's own conclusion is the procedure: name the register, and where no
graded law covers it let the ARITHMETIC decide how many commas to take, never a connective rule.

**S2. AN IMPORT FROM ANOTHER TRACK IS THE COSTLIEST MOVE I MAKE, and a law measured where nothing else could
mark the juncture does not survive a source that marks it.** PA part 14 (batch H1) is the clean instance: four
errors, all four false cuts, all four in the two hadith documents, and every one an NP-track hadith rule
imported into a track that had never graded it. H2's "the isnād→matn juncture is the ONE reliable cut" produced
the false cut at `أنه سمع أبا سعيد الخدري , [[يقول]] قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم " بينا أنا نائم …`, which
is ONE segment; H4's exception produced `قالوا فما أولت ذلك يا رسول الله قال " الدين " .`, also ONE segment.
H2 was measured on the de-punctuated track, where syntax was the only thing that could mark that juncture; on a
marked page the printed comma had already decided, and no comma was printed there. **CHECK: when a law's
evidence comes from a differently-marked source, re-derive it on the page in front of me or drop it.**

book sits on.** Graded twice on a label I stretched. PA part 11 (batch C3, PA34): E3(b)'s wire-profile licence
was graded on a Syrian-doctor dispatch (`وقد ولد في دمشق عام 1959 , ‖ وأمضى غالبية حياته`) and I "read 'profile'
as covering any life-story" — two false cuts on a children's-magazine biography. The 9B/9C pair says the same

false cuts) CUT nine times in a children's storybook one batch later (batch 9C, L9C.3 — missed

**S5. A ZONE IS A REGISTER, AND THE LAYOUT — NOT THE GENRE NAME — SAYS WHICH ONE I AM IN. A display line, a

**S6. A CORRECTION IS SCOPED TO THE REGISTER THAT PRODUCED IT: write the register name INSIDE the rule (D9),

**S7. THE PRE-COMMIT CHECK — the only thing that catches this class at the site.** At every boundary I have
drawn, name (i) the rule that licenses it and (ii) the register that rule was graded on. If (ii) is not the page

## PART 1 — MASTER LAW
**M1. What this corpus is.** Printed pages with punctuation and layout stripped. The gold boundary is where
the SOURCE broke a unit — a verse circle, a line end, a layout cell, a full stop — never "where syntax
permits a break." Every document I scored 100 on was a reconstruction of a page; every disaster was a parse
of a sentence. **Reconstruct the layout; do not parse the grammar.**
**M2. Order of operations, before reading a single connective.** (−1) name the canonical printed unit →
(0) block the document, assign each block a family → (1) topic → (2) register + MEASURED clause-length band →
(3) bracket every subordinate clause, fix its direction → (3.5) elaboration filter → (3.6) hinge filter →
(4) sort the remaining coordinators → (5) the family's pass. Steps 3–4 and the filters are family-A only.
**No connective policy crosses a document boundary.**
**M3. Step −1 is the whole game.** quran → āyah · bible → verse · poetry → printed line/bayt · chant → rhyming
phrase · exercise → slot · legal → article/enumerated line · hadith → isnād link then matn sentence · front
matter → field/line · subtitle sample → line. Name it in writing before anything else.
**M4. If a canonical unit exists it IS the law; syntax has no vote in either direction.** It cuts through an
iḍāfa, an أن-complement, a verb and its PP, a negation…بل pair, a frame and its quote; and nothing inside it
ever cuts, however many sentences, questions or turns it holds. Every filter below is then IRRELEVANT.
**M5. In family B1 a law that requires me to JUDGE is wrong.** Every perfect B1 document had a mechanical law
("cut at every fāṣila", "one per bayt", "one stem + one per option"); every failing one had a judgement word.
**M6. The three families.** **A — prose**: unit = the sentence. **B1 — canonical printed unit** (āyah, verse,
bayt, slot, article, field, imprint line): nothing shields, not even syntax; I either name the unit (F1 100)
or substitute a syntax/rhyme/sentence law (F1 60–83), no middle. **B2 — utterance/datum** (balloon dialogue,
credits, interjections), ~3–5 tok/seg: subordination shields, discourse relations do not; **I under-cut.**
**M7. What density is for.** (a) a check on my COUNT in family A, never a licence to place a cut; (b) the
family sorter where no canonical unit exists (under ~6 tok/seg → display/dialogue); (c) nothing where the
unit is canonical. **MEASURED key bands (graded keys, 9A–9C):** masthead ~3 · worksheet 2.5–6 · lyric line
2.7–4 · balloon 5.0 · subtitle line-list 5.9 · bayt ~6.5 · folk tale 8.4 · hadith 8.8–9.3 · Bible verse
10.4–14.5 · **novel / children's storybook 11.1–11.2** · literary short story 12.4 · classical argumentative
14.1 · children's magazine biography 14.1 · legal ~14.7 · translated literary 16.2 · news dispatch 16.4 ·
ornate appreciation 16.5 · popular-nonfiction essay 17.0 · magazine fact page 17.2 · school essay 11–18 ·
encyclopaedic 18.6–22.3 · translated academic ~20 · scholarly monograph ~25 · Qur'ān 4–17.
**M8. Where the logical unit and the printed line diverge, THE LINE WINS.** The smallest thing the printer
put on its own line. An over-long option splits at its wrap; two short phrases can share one slot; a URL
broken across a line is two data.
**M9. The pass, by family — run it BEFORE committing.** A: see D4 — and NEVER delete a ولا/ولم cut. B1:
neither delete nor split — **count** the units the page implies against my boundary count. B2: run the pass
in reverse and split.

## PART 2 — THE LAW BY TOPIC
### EXPOSITORY — 34 docs, F1 ~88. **My bias here is now OVER-cutting (batch 9: P 82 / R 93).**
**E1. THE DEFAULT AT A CONNECTIVE IS MERGE.** In modern, encyclopaedic, journalistic, translated or
scholarly Arabic prose a clause opened by a connective continues the printed sentence unless it is an
independent NEW assertion with its own new full-NP subject. Nine tenths of my false cuts are connective
cuts. When two readings are equally good, MERGE — and let the asyndetic boundaries carry the count.
**E2. THE KILLED LIST — these do NOT cut in modern/scholarly expository.** Killed in batch 9, each with
2–4 false cuts of evidence: **وقد / ولقد + perfect (never, not once)** · **حيث** · **إذ** · **كما / كما أن**
when it merely adds a comparison · **ولكن / ولكنا / ولكننا** · **غير أن / إلا أن** · **فإن / فإنه / فلذلك /
فالـ and every other inferential ف in MODERN prose** · **فقد** · **أما…فـ inside a running paragraph** ·
a bare و + a fronted PP or adverbial (ومن أبرزها · وبهذا · وفي هذا السياق) · و + a verb sharing the previous
subject (ويمكنهم · واستخدم · ويعتمد ذلك). What survives of the old E2: nothing. Rebuild from E3 outward.
**E2b. THE MASTER TEST.** At each candidate site ask: **is the next clause a CONSEQUENCE or ELABORATION of
the clause I am in (→ MERGE), or an INDEPENDENT DATUM / a new move (→ CUT)?** Not the connective, not the
token count. **But E2b is a tie-breaker, NOT a licence to merge:** batch 9C's "default MERGE" produced 57
missed cuts. Where the two readings are close, follow E2c and E2d, then check the count against M7.
**E2c. لكن / ولكن / لكنني ALWAYS OPENS a segment** (6 graded misses in 9C), and the segment it opens RUNS ON
into the following ف-clause. The ONLY exception: an explicit deny/concede→correct pair whose first half is
negated or conceded (`ولسنا نقصد أن … ولكنا نقول إن` · `فنحن نسلم … ولكننا نظن` · `كان قوي الفهم … لكنه
واجه`) — N4. KILLED: the "merge at لكن unless ~30 tokens" policy.
**E2d. The ف of CONSEQUENCE cuts in essay, expository, scholarly and TRANSLATED-literary prose** (8 graded
misses in one essay: فهي تلقي · فالتفاعل · فعبارة · فالكاتب · فثمة · فهو ما زال · فالأعراس · بل يخفي). It
merges only inside a FAST NARRATED ACTION RUN (`فأخذته وحبسته في غرفة ثم قالت` · `فطار فوق الشجرة وقال`).
Everywhere else treat ف as a med CUT. Same for **بل after a completed clause** and for **ثم opening a new
time-step** (`ثم نجوت … ‖ ثم همت`), which merges only inside one fast move.
**E3. WHAT DOES CUT.** (a) **Asyndeton** — a new finite clause with no connective starting a new topic (the
backbone, hi); but NOT a supporting comment (`يؤيد ذلك ما وصل إلينا`), NOT an appositive verdict on the same
subject (`ورجل دولة يعتبر مؤسس علم الاجتماع`), NOT a re-naming tail (`المعروف أكثر باسم ابن خلدون`), NOT a
comment on the object just named. In a PROFILE it cuts even after an indefinite head (`طبيب في الأربعينيات ‖
غادر سورية`); after a predicate ADJECTIVE it cuts (`هامور عبقري ‖ اكتسب خبرة`). (b) **و + an INDEPENDENT
DATUM** — a new full-NP subject, a new passive fact, or in a wire profile / an 11-token narrative simply a
new finite verb. (c) **وهو / وهي + a VERBAL clause** — but وهي + a bare NP predicate does NOT. (d) **و +
هناك / وتوجد existential.** (e) **وكذلك**, even heading a gapped PP. (f) **ولا / ولم / ولن + a new negated
predication** — cut BEFORE it. (g) **بل after a completed clause** (not بل inside a لم…بل pair).
(h) **حتى أن · ومن ثم · بينما · لكن (E2c) · ف of consequence (E2d)**. (i) **A direct QUESTION** in argument
and expository prose — but NOT in a hadith matn. (j) Every heading, section number, bare cross-reference
line, tale-closing formula, and bare PP quotation frame (`وفي محكم التنزيل ‖`).
**E4. Classical argumentative prose (key 14 tok/seg) — the unit is the STEP.** The inferential ف is the signal
but a COIN-FLIP, never hi. A three-to-six-token verdict is a segment (`وهو باطل ‖`). KILLED: "the classical
sentence runs from one discourse particle to the next."
**E5. Enumerative appositives stay INLINE.** `الأولى هي… والثانية هي…` / `أحدهما في… والآخر في…` are one
segment with the stem that promised them (4 false cuts); they cut only as full argumentative steps in
classical prose. A chain of predicates about the SAME subject runs on.
**E6. An أن / إن / بأن complement is shielded, وأن-links included** (3 false cuts) — **but the shield ends
where the writer resumes in his own voice** (`قال … أن … هيئة رئاسية جديدة ‖ وهناك مسعى للتوصل…`).
Reported-speech frames with a VERB weld forward (وقال · وأشار · وذكر · قائلا); a bare PP frame (وفي محكم
التنزيل) does NOT — it is its own line. حيث / إذ / لكن after a completed report do NOT cut.
**E7. ORNATE APPRECIATION IS FINE-GRAINED, ~16 tok/seg.** KILLED: "heritage articles run 18–25 and do not
cut" (R 69.8). Such prose is a STACK of short predications: cut before every group headed by a FINITE VERB
(`كاتب رشيق العبارة متين التركيب ‖ يطرب بأسلوبه … ‖ يبتكر بكتاباته … ‖ ينظم الشعر … ‖ ويفتخر به لأنه…`), but
an epithet run with NO finite verb is one segment (`مقر بضعفه صادق بحديثه مسامح…`).
### NARRATIVE — 16 docs, F1 86.2. Errors balanced; placement, not count.
**N1. Registers, MEASURED from graded keys.** folk tale **8.4** · novel / children's STORYBOOK **11.1–11.2** ·
literary short story **12.4** · children's magazine BIOGRAPHY feature **14.1** · translated literary **16.2**.
Measure before deciding (D7): narrative clusters at 11–12 far more often than at 14. In an 11-token register
**و + a new finite VERB is a new sentence** (وأيقظت زوجها ‖ وحكت له · وكبرت المولودة ‖ وأصبحت طفلة ·
ويذهبان معا إلى البحر ‖ وتحمل معه) — 9 graded misses. In a 14-token feature the same و merges.
**N2. The و/ف-sort in a narrated story.** The ف of consequence merges ONLY inside a fast action run
(فأخذته وحبسته · فطار فوق الشجرة وقال · فضربها بالعصا ونادى وبكى); a ف that starts a new MOVE cuts (E2d).
Mid-paragraph و + a fronted subordinate (ودون أن يحارب) merges. What always cuts: asyndeton at a topic
shift, و/ف + a fronted TEMPORAL phrase (وبعد أن، ولما، وقبل أن، وفي عام), a new named agent, لكن, ولن/ولم +
a new negated predication, a display line, a tale-closing formula (`وعاشوا في هناء وسرور وصفاء ‖`).
**N3. Filter 3.5 — elaboration is internal.** By reliability: a resumptive clitic ON the verb reaching back
(`يشقها`; a pronoun in a trailing PP does NOT shield) · a possessed-attribute subject · a deictic re-naming
chain · a comparison (كما · كـ · كأن) · an adverbial with no finite verb after it · a repeated verb · an
instantaneous reflex · a bare لكن reversal · **a backgrounding كان + لا يزال, which attaches BACKWARD**.
**N4. Filter 3.6 — a correlative pair is ONE segment. RESTORED with force after batch 9.** act→reaction ·
say→what was said · **deny→correct (لم…لكن · لسنا نقصد…ولكنا نقول · ليس…وإنما · لا…بل)** · define X→define
not-X · name→rank (وهي من أهم) · frame→deliver · label→name · هل…أم with any aside · the thwarted attempt.
**Correlation outranks length, continuation, AND every E-rule.** I have now broken this twice and paid twice.
**N5. Subordinator direction, decided before any coordinator.** BACKWARD (لأن · إذ · حيث · على حين · **في
حين** · مما · رغم أن · غير أن · بعدما · backward حتى) close the segment they follow — the boundary is at the
NEXT MAIN CLAUSE, and cutting at the subordinator costs a matched pair. FORWARD (حتى أن · ولما · وحين ·
وإذا · **بينما** · forward في حين "while some…others…") open the next segment and drag their consequence
chain in. **بينما moved to FORWARD on two graded misses** (`وكان ذلك بالمشاركة ‖ بينما كانت جانيت راولي…` ·
`محسوب على السعودية ‖ بينما خالد خوجا محسوب على قطر…`); في حين stayed BACKWARD on a graded false cut. Everything inside a subordinate scope or an أن-complement is shielded, its
internal و / ثم / وإنما included. **In family A a fronted adverbial opens a segment only if a finite clause
follows it — but in B2 it opens the next utterance regardless** (batch-9 error, third D3 repeat).
**N6. What always cuts.** Asyndeton between two predications with new topics · a bare asyndetic finite verb
with nothing reaching back · **every direct question, closing at the end of the WHOLE question** · every
change of speaking turn · a whole-utterance one-word cry or assent (ماذا · طبعا · نعم · كفى · هه · حسنا) ·
a formula/duʿāʾ/oath · an appositive following a COMPLETED predication · a heavy event-masdar series.
**N7. Frames weld forward; only display lines stand alone.** Verbal speech frame → the first sentence of its
quote · chant frame · list stem (نذكر منهم) · reply/address frame · a pre-posed vocative. **A vocative or a
NAME after a complete utterance closes with it, and on a display/balloon page it is its own line.**
### EXERCISE / WORKSHEET — 37 docs, F1 98.8. My strongest law; obey it mechanically.
**X1. Two kinds of segment and nothing else: the STEM and EACH OPTION.** One boundary inside a block (stem →
first option) plus one at the close of every option. Segments = blocks + total options; verify the count.
**X2. The STEM is the entire run from the end of the previous option to where the options begin** — ONE
segment however long and however narrative, swallowing scenarios, definitions, quoted Qur'ān/hadith with
verse numerals, speech frames, riddles, the marker `س`, and any commentary.
**X3. The stem absorbs every word printed ONCE that all options need and closes exactly where the options
begin to DIFFER, mid-phrase if need be** — a stranded or fused preposition, a copula, a quantifier, a
governing masdar, a head noun awaiting its complement, even a bare `ال`. This cuts inside an iḍāfa.
**X4. Option junctures are ASYNDETIC; a و is never a boundary** — spacing carries no information. Nor is
anything else clause-grade: not أو، حتى، حيث، لأن، أن، purpose لـ، a relative, a conditional.
**Check: read the token to the right of every cut; if it is و, delete the cut.**
**X5. Block shapes.** صح/خطأ are TWO options (block = 3); a three-option MCQ = 4; يعجبني / لا يعجبني = 2.
Solve the last, cleanest option first and let it place the ambiguous stem→first-option boundary.
**X6. The unit is the printed SLOT, not the logical option.** An over-long option splits at its wrap; two
short asyndetic phrases can share one slot; a combination item is partitioned by option COUNT; ~once per 100
boundaries the last option and the next stem share a line, flagged only by a ±1 discrepancy.
### HADITH — 6 docs. Rebuilt from two clean keys in 9B; measured **8.8–9.3 tok/seg**.
**H1. عن NEVER CUTS.** Cut ONLY before a قال that introduces a fresh حدثنا / حدثني. The whole عن-chain plus
the أن/عن-frame plus the honorific plus the questioner's setup is ONE segment: `حدثنا عبد الله بن محمد ‖ قال
حدثنا أبو عامر ‖ قال حدثنا سليمان بن بلال المديني عن ربيعة … عن زيد بن خالد الجهني أن النبي صلى الله عليه
وسلم سأله رجل عن اللقطة ‖ فقال …`. KILLED: the "further عن opens a link" rule (4 false cuts) and "4–6 tok/seg".
**H2. The isnād→matn juncture is the ONE reliable cut: right before the قال / فقال that opens the matn.**
Still KILLED: "isnād and matn form one sentence" (cost R 20–25).
**H3. The matn is COARSE, ~8–11 tokens per unit.** Cuts at a فذرها / فقال turn and at a فإن…ف conditional.
Does NOT cut: at a direct QUESTION (N6 does not reach a matn); at every ثم (`ثم عرفها سنة ثم استمتع بها ‖`);
at a transmitter's variant (`أو قال وعاءها`), which rides inside its unit.
**H4. A question and WHAT FOLLOWS IT merge — answer or reaction alike** (`قال فضالة الغنم قال لك أو لأخيك
أو للذئب ‖`). Exception: a formal `قال قالوا …` question answered by a fresh `قال` + the ruling DOES split.
### QURAN — 7 docs, F1 98.7.
**Q1. The āyah is the segment, exhaustively; boundary count = verse count.** It cuts through an appositive
relative, an istithnāʾ, a ḥāl accusative, a bare opening preposition, and a pair of verses forming one
sentence; it holds together three or more predications, interior questions, imperatives, 33-token stretches.
No connective in a muṣḥaf is clause-grade — only the fāṣila.
**Q2. The rhyme is the punctuation.** A candidate boundary not on the fāṣila is wrong; a fāṣila I do not cut
at is a missed boundary. Waqf signs are recitation guidance, never boundaries. The basmala welds FORWARD
into āyah 1. **An āyah quoted inside prose is still indivisible** — its internal و / إذ / ولكن never cut.
### BIBLE — 10 docs, F1 94.3; the five most recent all 100.0.
**B1. The unit is the verse, exhaustively; count = verse count; zero intra-verse cuts, zero merges.**
Detector without numerals: ~11–14 tokens, and **the printed full stop tends to fall in the MIDDLE of a verse
while the verse end falls at a juncture my prose rules call comma-grade** — near the 12-token mark prefer
the comma-grade juncture. A genealogy's three-verse cycle is a metronome.
**B2. Nothing inside a verse cuts** — not a printed full stop, not a direct question, not a change of
speaking turn, not a one-word cry, not a vocative, not a بل-correction, not an asyndetic new predication.
**B3. Every verse end cuts, however mid-sentence it lands** — between a frame and its quote, before قائلا,
inside a descriptive tail, inside a same-subject و-chain, before لأن, before كما. **The verse boundary
outranks the forward-welding frame law.**
### POETRY — 5 docs, F1 86.2 (P 83.1); I over-cut.
**P1. The unit is the printed line** — for a two-hemistich poem the BAYT, both hemistichs, closing on the
rhyme (proved by tadwīr). A sung LYRIC is not in hemistichs and runs 2–5 words per line: it has a refrain and
short breath-lines, where a qaṣīda has a constant metre and one rhyme to the end.
**P2. Rhyme is NOT a boundary detector, only a confirmation once line lengths are known.** Internal rhyme —
tasrīʿ, the echo children's lyrics use constantly — is ordinary and does not cut.
**P3. Nothing shields inside a line and nothing survives across one.** The line splits a verb from its PP and
a negation from its بل-correction; a question inside a line does not close it. Across a line everything cuts.
**A line-initial و is never the cut: the cut is the word BEFORE it.**
**P4. Fix the REFRAIN/CHANT first, and cut FINE — a 2–4-token line is normal.** KILLED twice: "the most
conservative longest-line reading" (9C) and "an internal rhyme pair sits inside one line" (9D). **In a
children's chant/lyric the internal rhyme IS the break: split at every rhyming phrase whenever the two halves
are grammatically PARALLEL** — `قال سلام ‖ يا تمام ‖ … عمن نقروا ‖ عمن حفروا ‖ رسم نافر ‖ شكل غائر ‖ نقش
وسم ‖ رقش وشم ‖`. Only where the two rhyme words are separated by other material does one line hold both
(`أنا الطير الأخضر في قبة العسكر ‖`). A chant frame (وقال) welds onto line 1; bare poem titles stand alone.
**P5. A qaṣīda's bayt runs 9–10 tokens, not 6.5** (ʿAntara's muʿallaqa, 89 abyāt at 9.3, F1 100).
### CONVERSATIONAL / BALLOON — 4 docs, F1 ~92; I under-cut.
**C1. Balloon dialogue (B2, 3.5–5 tok/seg): every complete predication is a segment.** Turn change closes
one; with no speaker tags, gender agreement is the tracker. **In a BALLOON a one-word cry, laugh, assent or
marker has its own bubble and is a whole utterance** — هاها · هه · حسنا · وجدتها · بست · فرصة · مبروك.
**But in a SUBTITLE / LINE-LIST (B2, ~5–6 tok/seg) the interjection SHARES its line with what follows**
(`تبا المعذرة ‖` · `نعم قال بأنه يفضل…‖` · `لا لقد تفقدت الرشاشات ‖` · `أجل أنا ‖ أعني لديهم…‖`) — 4 graded
false cuts. A repeated formula is two utterances; an apology + its reason is ONE (`آسف يا مولاتي عندي
اجتماع في المساء ‖`).
**C2. ONLY syntactic subordination shields — and لأن counts as subordination here.** Shielding: أن/ما/إن
complements, relatives, كما, **explanatory لأن** (batch-9 correction), a clitic ON the verb, a protasis + ف
apodosis, هل…أم, gapped coordination, a pre-posed vocative. NOT shielding: elaboration, appositive
re-naming, correlative pairing, coordinated-NP series, possessed-attribute description — those SPLIT.
**A fronted time adverbial (الآن) opens the NEXT utterance even before a non-finite clause.**
**C3. A magazine service page (letters, Q&A, mailbag) is the finest register I have met** (R 66.7 once). Every
rubric, reader question, the reader's NAME and — separately — their city+country line, every listed title, and
every proposition of the editor's reply is its own segment.
**C4. RANK TEST — a title and its immediate SUBLINE are ONE segment** (3 confirmations): series title +
episode title · poster headline + subline · presenter banner + the title it presents (`كسلان جدا يقدم بكل فخر
ليلة بعد ليلة بعد ليلة ‖`) · bare label + its value · price + the slogan on its line. Same RANK → split.
### LEGAL — 1 doc, F1 97.6.
**L1. The unit is the constitutional line:** heading line, article rubric, article body, or one enumerated
item. `الباب/الفصل X` + its title is ONE segment WHEN THEY SHARE A LINE; in a BOOK the chapter ordinal is
stacked ABOVE its title and they are two segments. Asyndeton starts a new article.
**L2. Inside an article body و / ف / كما CONTINUE the segment.** أما does NOT cut, و + a fronted adverbial
does NOT cut, a bare fronted time adverbial attaches to the clause that FOLLOWS it, an inline comma-series
inside one item is not sub-segmented.
**L3. Cut immediately before the FIRST list item even if the stem breaks off mid-phrase, and once per item.**
Stems ending in الآتية / ما يلي / ومنها close a segment and do not get one of their own.
### FRONT MATTER / MASTHEAD / IMPRINT — a zone in any topic (B1, ~3 tok/seg).
**F1. The unit is the FIELD: every self-contained datum splits — in a STACKED COLUMN of fields.** The
masthead NAME alone is a segment (`ماجد ‖`). Strapline | magazine name; two calendars = two datelines; `السنة
… ‖ العدد … ‖ الثمن … ‖`; a Latin repeat strip repeats the fields; valediction | signature. **But a running
FOOTER STRIP is ONE segment** — a school-reader glossary printed as one band is a single unit however many
headword/gloss pairs it holds (`غائر عميق مجوف نافر بارز مغاور حمع مغارة … الرقش التزيين ‖`, 5 false cuts).
The tell: no per-line label morphology, entries running on horizontally.
**F2. A function LABEL KEEPS ITS VALUE — one segment.** KILLED (3 false cuts): "the bare label gets its own
segment". Key: `تأليف باتريسيا ديلبيانو ‖ ترجمة أماني فوزي حبشي ‖ مراجعة عز الدين عناية ‖`; also `يكتبها محمد
أمين ‖ يرسمها علاء الدين سعد ‖` and `إعداد مجد الدين فهمي ‖`. But a **bare NUMBER on its own line IS its own
segment** (`… العبودية الحديثة ‖ 1 ‖ العبيد والخدم …`). A publisher name still splits from its registration
clause; an email or URL splits at every printed break; a footnote marker closes the sentence it annotates.
**F3. Test for two adjacent display data.** If the second is a SUBLINE, a value, or a slogan sharing the
first's line → ONE segment. If each is a complete item of the same RANK → TWO. A bare numeral or ordinal on
its own line → its own segment. A dispatch entry (place + correspondent) is one; a signature name and the
address line beneath it are two.

## PA APPENDIX — punctuated-track law (graded)
Written after batch A (mean F1 92.93: exercise 99.2 · wiki 98.8 · imprint+preface 94.1 · classical 87.1 ·
magazine page 85.5). Everything above still holds; this appendix only says WHAT THE MARKS DO. Governing
finding of batch A: **the mark fixes the INDEX, and for one mark only does it also fix the FACT.**

**PA0. Measured PA densities (words per segment, back-computed from the graded P/R).** translated academic
preface + imprint **15.1** · encyclopaedic wiki **16.2** · classical philosophical prose **17.5** · children's
magazine sports page **14.3** · MCQ worksheet **5.5**. Note the classical band is 17.5, NOT the 14.1 of E4 —
I cut to 15.4 and lost 12 precision points. PA prose clusters at **14–18** whatever the century.

**PA1. THE PERIOD IS ABSOLUTE. Every single `.` token is a boundary.** Not "every sentence-final period" —
EVERY period. The annotators cut inside initials, inside e-mails, inside URLs: `Robert L . ‖ Rothstein and
Daniel D . ‖ Perlmutter ‖`, `hindawi @ hindawi . ‖ org ‖`, `https : / / www . ‖ hindawi . ‖ org ‖`. I ruled
those five "not marks" and took five straight misses. The ONLY exception found is a decimal point between two
digits: `( 131 . 5 )` does not cut. **A RUN of two or more consecutive dots is ONE mark, not N marks** — the
worksheet blank `. . . . . . . .` carries at most the stem boundary, on its LAST dot; the magazine's `. .`
carries at most one boundary, on the SECOND dot.

**PA2. `؟` and `!` are absolute too** — 10/10 in batch A, including a dash-parenthetical rhetorical question
(`- كيف يمكن للمرء ألا يكون مهتما بها ؟ ‖ - ولكن بخلفيتين…`), a chain of quiz-style questions, and `!` closing
a dedication. Never merge a printed question mark.

**PA3. `؛` DEFAULTS TO CUT — 12 of the 15 in the one document that had them.** KILLED, on three misses, my
batch-A rule "؛ cuts only where an INDEPENDENT new predication follows". The key cuts before a bare PP
(`وليف ؛ ‖ مع أمل في مستقبل أفضل`), before an appositive accusative inside dashes (`كلنا ؛ ‖ أفرادا ودولا
ومؤسسات دولية -`) and before an appositive NP (`شيئا ذا قيمة ؛ ‖ شيئا يختلف عن التحليلات`). It does NOT cut
in three shapes only: (a) the explicative **أي** (`هذا الكتاب ؛ أي كيف سيتعامل`); (b) a following
**subordinator**, لأن above all (`جسما منقسما ؛ لأن هذه القوة…` — and the real boundary there is UNMARKED,
eight tokens earlier, before the ف); (c) the **second member of an enumeration the stem already announced**
(`استراتيجية ذات مسارين : مسار " طبيعي " … ؛ ومسار " غير طبيعي "` — my one false cut in that document, and
E5 had already told me so). A حال participle after ؛ also merges (`وجهتي نظر متداخلتين ؛ واضعة في الاعتبار`).

**PA4. `:` NEVER CUTS inside a line — 12/12.** Label + value is one field (`تليفون : + ٤٤ …`, `تصميم الغلاف :
خالد المليجي`); the list stem keeps its first item (`الأسئلة الفلسفية الكلاسيكية : هل من الممكن … ؟ ‖`,
`إلى ثلاثة عصور : الفلسفة القديمة … والفلسفة الحديثة .`); the speech-frame colon welds forward (`ونقول : كان
مؤتمر كوبنهاجن مخيبا للآمال , لقد ضاع كل شيء . ‖`); `ومنها : هوسة بم درادم` rides inside its sentence.
A colon is a boundary only when a `<NL>` follows it.

**PA5. THE COMMA IS THE HARD ONE: it cuts before a NEW MOVE with a finite verb, and this cost me 13 of my 21
recall errors.** It CUTS at: `,` + **ولكن / ولكننا** (`في ذلك الاحترار , ‖ ولكننا لسنا مقتنعين`, `على طول
الطريق , ‖ ولكن التغييرات لن تكون`); `,` + **و + a new finite verb opening a new assertion** (`باستمرار , ‖
وتظهر معلومات جديدة`, `اقتصاد طاقة جديد , ‖ وسوف تحدث " صدمات "`, `التنوير , ‖ وتشمل شخصيات مثل هيجل`,
`غربية قوية , ‖ وتشمل أعمال موسى مندلسون`, `في بريطانيا , ‖ وانتشرت في أنحاء عديدة`, `فوق اللوح , ‖ ويحاول
التجديف`, `للتوجيه , ‖ ويكون وزن وطول اللوح`, `الألعاب الخشنة , ‖ ويمكن أن يلعبها الشباب`); `,` + **وهذا /
وبالنسبة + a predication** (`للناخب التقليدي , ‖ وهذا له آثار واضحة`, `وغيرها ) , ‖ وبالنسبة إليهم يمكن`);
`,` + **an asyndetic finite verb** (`من ستة لاعبين , ‖ يفصل كل مجموعة`); `,` + **حيث + finite verb**
(`استقبال سموه لهم , ‖ حيث أهدى نجوم الفريق`) — حيث is still BACKWARD as a shield (N5) but the printed comma
in front of it is a boundary; `,` + **a fronted adverbial that carries its own verb** (`بين الفريقين , ‖ وعلى
خط الوسط يقف الحكم…`). It does NOT cut at: `,` + **مثل / كـ** (`مدركة بالفعل , مثل العين…`, `به بعد , مثل من
خلق مكفوف البصر`, and unmarked `وحنت إليه مثل من كان يصيرا`); `,` + a **purpose لـ** (`نادي الشباب , ليتحقق
الفوز`, `للمرة الأولى , ليتوج نادي " العين "`, `البطولة الآسيوية … , ليأتي فوزهم`); `,` + an **apodosis** after
a fronted protasis (`جميع الأشياء , أراد أن يعلم`, `بطولة الدوري , فازوا بالمركز الثالث`); `,` + a **حال
participle** (`ينطلق المتزلج , مستلقيا على بطنه`); `,` + an **explicative** (`بين اللاعبين , بمعنى أن الفرقة`,
`الحماسية , ومنها :`); `,` + **حتى** (`ويترك المصنوع , حتى اشتد شوقه`); `,` inside a coordinated NP or a PP
series. At `,` + و + finite verb the key still goes both ways about half the time (`نشأت في جزر هاواي ,
وتمارس الآن` merges; `وتقام … مباريات , وتمنح النقاط` merges but `, ويحدد في بعض الحالات` cuts) — that residue
is D8 noise; settle it with E2b (new datum → cut, same move continuing → merge) and invent no further rule.

**PA6. UNMARKED BOUNDARIES ARE REAL, and they sit exactly where the de-punctuated doctrine already put them.**
Graded: before the **ف of consequence** (`قوى شائعة في الأجسام ومنقسمة بانقسامها ‖ فهي لذلك لا تدرك` — E2d,
and the printed ؛ eight tokens later is NOT the boundary); before **وإنما** (`فإنها ليست حقيقة ذاته ‖ وإنما
حقيقة ذاته ذلك الشيء`); between **parallel short predications in a series** (`والذوق يدرك الطعوم ‖ واللمس يدرك
الأمزجة`). So: never let the absence of a mark stop a cut my doctrine demands, and never let the presence of a
comma force one.

**PA7. The `<NL>` rule is free marks — take all of them and nothing else on that line.** Every display line,
heading (`= = مقدمة = =`), field, byline, dedication signature and worksheet block closes on its last token;
inside such a line the label keeps its value (F2 confirmed 12/12) and nothing sub-segments.

**PA8. In a magazine the `. .` pause is NOT automatically the sentence.** Graded: it cuts between two complete
narrative moves (`للمباراة النهائية . . ‖ حقق لاعبو الفريق`) and between a caption's topic and its rank
(`فريق نادي العين . . ‖ بطل كأس صاحب السمو`), but it MERGES topic→comment (`" لونجو " . . هي الآن أعظم امرأة`,
`الهوسة . . لعبة شعبية جماعية`, `رفع الأثقال للنساء . . ستصبح رياضة أولمبية`), protasis→apodosis (`فإذا تمكن
… . . يعتبر هذا اللاعب خاسرا`), a purpose لـ (`بالهدف الذهبي . . ليختتم العيناويون`), a trailing PP (`الفرقة
" أ " . . من أجل إكمال اللعب`) and a REPEATED CHANT (`ومنها : هوسة بم درادم . . هوسة بم درادم ‖` is ONE
segment — KILLED my P4 "a repeated formula is two utterances" for a chant quoted inside running prose).

**PA9. The worksheet law is unchanged and still my best (99.2).** Stem + each option; the printed dot-run of
the blank belongs to the STEM and the stem boundary sits on its last dot; a stem ending in `:` or `-` closes
on that mark. **X6 restated: an option is ONE slot however long** — `( ب ) أن يكون نظام الحكم فيها ملكيا
دستوريا برئاسة الأمير عبدالله بن الحسين ‖` is one segment; I split it at دستوريا and that was my only pair of
errors in 120 boundaries.

**PA10. NEW FAILURE MODE — D10: I let the density band override the marks in the wrong direction.** In the
classical text I computed 46 w/seg from the full stops alone, correctly concluded the full stop was not the
whole story, then over-corrected to 15.4 against a gold of 17.5, cutting at eight commas and unmarked sites
the key merged (مثل twice, an apodosis, a protasis, a ف-clause, an appositive). **CHECK: when the mark-only
count is off band, add boundaries ONLY at the shapes PA5/PA6 licence — ولكن, و + a new finite assertion, an
asyndetic finite verb, ف of consequence, وإنما, parallel series members — and stop at the LOW end of the band
(17–18 for prose), not the middle.**
