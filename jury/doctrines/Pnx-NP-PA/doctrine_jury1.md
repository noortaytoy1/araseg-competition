## STANDING ORDER 0 — REGISTER SCOPING (train-graded)

**A graded law carries force ONLY inside the register that graded it.** Every rule in PARTS 1–3 and in the appendices below was
bought with corrections on documents of ONE register. Outside that register a law has no standing until a correction inside the
new register gives it one. This is the most repeated shape of error in my whole train record, and it is symmetrical: the same
seam has cost me false cuts when I carried a law one way and misses when I carried it the other. Run this section before PART 1,
on every document.

- **SO0.1. EVERY LAW WEARS ITS REGISTER LABEL, AND A LAW STATED WITHOUT ONE IS A TRAP.** Batch 1 (three `expository` documents)
  gave me RULE 1 — *"the annotators cut before لكن / ولكن, always"* — 4/4 there. Batch 2's narrative/literary pair reversed the
  sign at 11 of 14 sites, 8 false cuts on the literary document alone: `رقد باعوم على فراشه ‖ لكنه لم يغمض له جفن` — key joins;
  `فهم نماذج عدة تبلغ المئات ‖ ولكنهم مشمولون جميعا في رجولة واحدة` — key joins. Batch 2's own overriding lesson states the
  principle: *"Connective behaviour is REGISTER-DEPENDENT and flips sign… A rule without a register label is a trap."* Same for
  فـ (batch-1 RULE 3 against batch-2 N3), and batch-3 N12 scoped even batch-1 RULE 3a to expository on one missed cut —
  `حتى لو لم يتحدث أحد عنه مع الطفلة النحيلة ‖ فهي تعلم بداخلها أن والدها البارع لن يعود`.
  *Check:* I may not apply a rule until I can name the register that graded it AND the register of the stretch in front of me,
  and the two match.

- **SO0.2. SCOPING IS NOT DELETION — THE PARENT REGISTER KEEPS THE LAW.** Batch-3 N11 was right that إذ never cuts under the
  `narrative` label, 3/3 false cuts (`كان والدها غائبا عن المنزل ‖ إذ كان طيارا يشارك في الحرب في أفريقيا` — key joins). I then

## PART 1 — WHAT THIS CORPUS IS, AND THE ORDER I WORK IN

**This is not one segmentation task. It is three, and the topic label tells me which.** What was stripped
from the source differs in each, and the annotator can only reproduce what was actually there.
- **A. CANONICAL-UNIT SOURCES** — `quran`, `bible`, `poetry`, `hadith`. The source already carries one unit per line (ayah,
  verse, بيت); syntax is inoperative. I score 100.0 trusting the unit, ~85 adding stops to it.
- **B. GRIDS AND PRINTED LAYOUTS** — `exercise` and exam papers wholesale, plus list pages, tables, rubrics and label+value
  blocks embedded in *any* topic. The unit is the printed CELL: the annotator is reproducing stripped line breaks, colons and
  answer blanks, never syntax.
- **C. PROSE REGISTERS** — `expository`, `narrative`, `legal`, `conversational`. The unit is the clause-level orthographic
  sentence and the connective table decides — but its signs FLIP by register.

**Order of operations, every document, no exceptions:**
1. Read the LABEL, then the first 40 words. The label picks the family, not the subject matter (a popular-science column
   labelled `narrative` obeys narrative law; an Arabic-lesson page is a grid).
2. Ask **PROSE OR GRID** before reading a single connective. Grid tells: repeated short rows, option runs, `صح`/`خطأ`, rubric
   verbs, table headers, numbered items, label+value pairs, verse rhyme, hemistichs.
3. Family A or B → find the unit, resolve it to the **finest thing the page prints**, switch the connective table OFF, and stop.
   Family C → load that register's table and walk every clause-initial connective.
4. Segment in STRETCHES, not documents: a magazine page is half prose and half list, a worksheet has prose islands, a diary
   carries layout lines. Decide each stretch's shape on its own.
5. Count words per unit against the band (M8); outside it, re-walk. Then verify the units partition ALL the words and the last
   unit ends on the last word.

### Master laws — they outrank every connective rule in Part 2
- **M1. GRID OUTRANKS SYNTAX, AND MUST BE RESOLVED TO ITS FINEST PRINTED GRAIN.** In a grid the structural unit is the ONLY
  boundary generator: the connective table neither creates nor cancels a boundary. Test: *would this get its own row on the
  page?* For an answer option, a bank word, a header cell, a numbered label, a verse, a بيت — yes. Reading a grid coarsely is my
  largest error class ever (225 recall points).
- **M2. NEVER ADD SYNTAX ON TOP OF A STRUCTURAL UNIT, AND LET EVERY UNIT EDGE CUT THROUGH THE SYNTAX.** Inside the unit, the
  edition's full stops, speech turns, questions, vocatives, imperatives and بل are invisible; at the edge, mid-PP-list,
  mid-construct and even frame-from-quotation all cut. Breaking the first half cost 42 precision points in one batch.
- **M3. THE ARITHMETIC-COINCIDENCE ALARM.** If my model reaches the right TOTAL by adding N unit-internal cuts and subtracting N
  structural ones, it has the right unit and is corrupting it. Fall back to the pure unit.
- **M4. IN PROSE THE BOUNDARY GOES IMMEDIATELY BEFORE THE ELEMENT THAT OPENS THE NEW UNIT** — the connective, the fronted
  adverbial, the first verb of an action run. Direction test: *backward-facing* → boundary after it (إذ، من بينها partitive، أما
  + anaphoric echo، one actor's next sub-step، a postposed vocative، a trailing adverb); *forward-facing* → boundary before it
  (فهو/فهي/فهذا، فقد + perfect، a fronted PP، a vocative، asyndeton، و + a new subject). **Positional tie-breaking is dead** —
  "take the earlier" and "take the later" junction each failed a whole batch. **Two items were deleted from the backward list in
  batch 8: وهذا/وذلك + a summary or cause CUTS (X6), and a bare صفة/asyndetic verb clause on a completed nominal predication
  CUTS (X7).**
- **M5. REGISTER BEFORE CONTENT.** لكن، فـ، إذ، حيث، أما، كما flip sign between registers; a connective rule without a register
  label is a trap.
- **M6. ASYNDETON + a re-stated explicit subject or a topic jump is the strongest boundary in the language**, in every register.
  Zero errors at these sites in six batches.
- **M7. SUBORDINATION HOLDS A UNIT TOGETHER HOWEVER LONG IT RUNS, and never cut inside a relative clause:** التي / الذي / من
  الموصولة، أن، لأن، كي / لـ، لو / إذا / لولا، بينما، عندما / حينما، متى، وكأن، temporal حتى، comparative كما. **Length alone
  NEVER licenses a cut.** Six batches, zero errors; every time I broke it I was wrong.
- **M8. DENSITY IS A GATE, NOT A NOTE — BUT ONLY IF THE BAND IS RIGHT. A WRONG BAND MANUFACTURES FALSE CUTS.** Words per unit:
  **expository-LAYOUT-PAGE (newspaper/magazine, masthead+byline+headline+boxes) 14–16 · expository-CONTINUOUS-PROSE (book,
  essay, preface, encyclopedia) 18–24 · NARRATIVE splits four ways: comic-strip balloons ≈4 · informal diary/testimony ≈8.8 ·
  literary prose & Nights ≈11–12 · fairy tale / children's abridgement 13–16 · bible 11–14.5 · exercise/worksheet 2.5–6 ·
  exam MCQ 4–4.5 · conversational ≈4 · legal ≈ provision length · quran and the `poetry` LABEL: count verses/lines, no
  density.** Above the band → missing cuts; below → inventing them. **Two batches running I was wrecked by a band that lumped
  sub-registers together** — "expository 13–16" cost 128 false cuts, "narrative 8.4–12.3" cost 75 more. Pick the SUB-register
  band first, and NEVER use a band to justify adding cuts.
- **M9. EVERY LAYOUT ELEMENT IS ITS OWN UNIT**, in every topic: title, headline, masthead, rubric, byline, translator line,
  dateline, genre tag, caption, table header cell, label+value line, **a bare date range used as a heading (`من عام 1929 حتى
  1949 ‖`)**, **a book's MAIN title separately from its SUBTITLE**, and **a bare SPEAKER LABEL in a script (V14)**. Two limits:
  a **chapter title + the section subtitle under it are ONE unit** (`السنوات الأولى في فلورنسا التعليم والتكوين ‖`) — only the
  numbered chapter/lesson LABEL splits off (E8); and **when a headline is a verbless nominal predication it ENDS there**
  (`ضرب الحكام ظاهرة خطيرة ‖ تزايدت في ملاعب…`) — never swallow the next verb into it.
- **M10. SHORTNESS AND REPETITION NEVER GLUE, AND STRIPPING ARTEFACTS ARE NOT DOUBTS.** `ثم انحرف يسارا`, `صح`, `علم`, `لماذا`,
  a bare numeral, eight identical `عليه السلام` cells — each its own unit. A dangling numeral or lone `م` at a boundary is a
  stripped symbol, not a reason to doubt the cut.
- **M11. RESIDUAL NOISE IS REAL — take the rule's majority side and move on.** One site each way is not a discriminator; never
  invent an exemption to explain a single site (my batch-8 "بل cuts in expository" exemption was 0/6).

## PART 2 — PER-TOPIC LAW

### expository — family C. TWO SUB-REGISTERS; decide which BEFORE reading a connective (M8)
**(a) LAYOUT PAGE** — newspaper/magazine page, masthead + byline + headline + boxes + ordinal subheads · **14–16 w/unit**.
**(b) CONTINUOUS AUTHORED PROSE** — book chapter, essay, author's preface, Wikipedia/encyclopedia article · **18–24 w/unit**.
In (b) **THE UNIT IS THE PERIOD, NOT THE CLAUSE.** Build ~20-word periods from the TOPIC structure; connectives only say where
an already-required boundary goes. My 128-false-cut batch was clause-level segmentation of period-level text.
- **X1. و + FINITE JOINS (~75%) in (b). This reverses what I believed for six batches** — 25/25 sites rode in one batch. The
  و-chain IS the sentence. **Cut at a و-clause only once the period has run its ~20 words and a new TOPIC opens**
  (`…في حرب 1967 ‖ وبدأ عملية… وأصدر مجموعة…` — وشن joined, وبدأ cut, وأصدر joined: rhythm, not connective identity).
  *In (a), and in translated modern academic prose at ≈14 w/unit, و+finite DOES cut — that is what the band is for.*
- **X2. فـ + FINITE JOINS** — a whole consequence chain (فرحت أبحث… فلم أجد ضالتي فتأكدت… وتوقفت) is ONE period. **فـ + a NOMINAL
  subject still CUTS** (فإننا · فإنهم · ففريق · فإن الخلايا · فهو · فهي · فالبعض · ففي سنة 1921 — 9/9). فقد + perfect is a **coin
  flip** (4/4): join while the period is short, cut once it has run long.
- **X3. DEAD AND REVERSED. NO CONTRAST OR CONSEQUENCE CONNECTIVE CUTS IN EXPOSITORY.** Ride لكن / ولكن / لكنه (10/10), **بل
  (6/6 — بل never cuts in ANY register)**, في حين أن, بينما, مع أن, إلا أن, غير أن continuing one argument, ولهذا / ولذلك /
  وبالتالي / كذلك (5/5), and **ثم + a new event** (5/5 — V6 is narrative-only). Still cut at fronted **أما … فـ …**, and see X8.
- **X4.** و + a verb of saying re-launches an attributed statement and cuts (وقال، وأعلن، وأشار، ويقول — 7/7). فعلى سبيل المثال
  opens a unit. **حتى قال المؤرخون إن…** likewise opens one — an attribution outranks the bare-resultative-حتى join.
- **X5. كما splits two ways.** **ADDITIVE كما** ("likewise/also", a new independent assertion) **CUTS**: كما يوجد · كما يغالي ·
  كما سيشارك · كما وصل · كما اقترب · كما تعلم · كما تشمل. **COMPARATIVE كما** pointing at a named parallel case **JOINS**:
  كما قدر سلاح جلوكوس · كما فعل جورج أرويل · وكما في تلك الروايات · كما أن + the same verb repeated.
- **X6. وذلك + a CAUSE is its own unit; وذلك/وتلك/وهذا + anything else RIDES.** CUTS: `…البطولة الثالثة عشرة ‖ وذلك بسبب وجود
  العديد من الأحداث…`; also وهما / وكلها + a fresh evaluative predication in scholarly prose (`…من عناصر الحياة ‖ وهما متلازمان…`,
  `…والأضرحة ‖ وكلها موشاة بزخارف فائقة ‖`). **JOINS (4 false cuts in one batch): وذلك + a TIME / PLACE / BENEFICIARY PP**
  (`وذلك في آخر تعاملات الأسبوع`, `وذلك لإنسان عاش 14 عاما`), **وتلك هي + an identification**, and **وهذا + a tag evaluating the
  clause it sits on** (`…ملايين السياح كل عام وهذا دليل كبير على تطورها`).
- **X8. إذ CUTS in expository** (`وتصدر هذه الترشيحات فيلم جاذبية ‖ إذ حصل على 11 ترشيحا ‖`) — V7's "إذ NEVER cuts" is
  narrative-only. This one clause of the dead X3 survives.
- **X7.** A bare apposed nominal clause describing the thing just named is its own unit (`…في غرب المحيط الهادي ‖ مساحتها 1041
  كيلو مترا مربعا ‖`), and so is an **asyndetic bare-verb clause after a complete nominal predication** (`…هو ثاني رؤساء مصر ‖
  تولى السلطة من سنة 1956 حتى وفاته ‖`) — do NOT ride these backward as صفة under M4. In an enumeration of full verbless
  predications (`على X + predicate`) each member is a unit; coordinated maṣdars inside one member are not.

### narrative — family C. **FOUR SUB-REGISTERS (M8): balloons ≈4 · diary/testimony ≈8.8 · literary & Nights ≈11–12 ·
fairy tale / children's abridgement 13–16.** Name it first; the old single 8.4–12.3 band cost 75 false cuts.
- **V1. THE CHAIN-HEAD LAW — my costliest habit, violated in five consecutive batches.** Cut BEFORE the FIRST verb of a
  coordinated action run, then never cut inside the run. Gluing the head backward and cutting mid-run costs a false cut AND a
  miss at the same site.
- **V2. A run ENDS at any change of LOCATION, STATE, SUBJECT or PERCEPTION.** A sub-step is a move within one bounded act at ONE
  place; a journey is not one run (`فخرج ‖ ونزل ‖ وسار` = three units). **Exception: a BIOGRAPHICAL life-story chain is one
  run** — و / ثم + the next phase of one life, or of one entity's history, joins.
- **V3. و + finite is ≈50/50 and the RHYTHM of the sub-register decides, not the connective.** Exemptions that always join: a
  non-finite conjunct; a verbless parallel attribute; a و-linked negative run; a genuine sub-step (V2); anything inside a list
  item or layout line; a two-member parallel pair (V8). No ad-hoc "it merely completes the thought".
- **V4. فـ + finite JOINS — 11/11 false cuts in batch 8B** (فأسفرت، فسأل، فلا تشكر، فأين، فلم تجب، فاستملحت…). So do **ثم**
  (`بداهة وطواعية ثم نكتة`, `رأسها تفكر ثم قالت`), **وإنما**, **ولكن** (`وتذبذبها في الوطنية ولكني لا أذكر`) and **و + a bare
  ḥāl** (`شخوص الروايات وهي تصغي إليه`, `وهي مشغولة بكساء` — this REVERSES V12 for a bare ḥāl). **فـ + a NOMINAL subject still
  cuts** (فهو، فهي، فهذا، فنظرا). فقد + perfect: coin flip. **In narrative, و + finite is ≈50/50 and RHYTHM decides it, not the
  connective** — target ≈10 w/unit and cut where a new event/actor opens.
- **V5. ولكن is a coin-flip decided by what FOLLOWS.** JOIN a bare 2–4 word reversal of the clause before it, and any balanced
  contrast pair (negation on one side, same clitic subject on both). CUT when it brings new content, its own complement, a new
  time-frame, a new question or a new speech turn.
- **V6. ثم + a distinct NEW EVENT cuts;** ثم inside an actor's run or narrating the next phase joins, and the asyndetic clause
  after a joined ثم does cut.
- **V7. The narrative subordinator table.** إذ **NEVER** cuts in narrative (but DOES in expository — X8). Resultative حتى cuts
  only as **حتى أنَّ**; bare حتى joins. فإذا / وإذا "and lo" and a conditional pair are ONE unit (4/4 in the Nights). حيث + a
  new OBSERVATION cuts, حيث elaborating the noun just named joins; coordinated subjunctive أنْ-complements under ONE order JOIN,
  coordinated أنَّ + a full proposition CUTS.
- **V8. A TWO-member parallel pair JOINS** — same frame, same subject or a cleft/modal echo, no new event (كان…وكانت,
  لعله…ولعله). A third member makes it a list and V11 takes over.
- **V9. Asyndetic juxtaposition or repetition of a NARRATIVE finite verb or noun = one unit EACH** (`يكبر ‖ يكبر ‖ يتضخم`),
  while the و-linked clauses around them join. Slogan: **و joins, asyndeton cuts.**
- **V10. MATCH THE LOCAL RHYTHM — a passage-level, not junction-level, decision.** ACTION passage → cut at every bare verb.
  DESCRIPTIVE passage → run the whole period. **Require a new EVENT or TOPIC, not merely a new predication.** In the INFORMAL
  DIARY sub-register the key cuts finely (≈8.8) and even لكن cuts there — I lost 20 recall points merging fragments.
- **V11. PROSE LISTS: an item earns its own unit when it contains an embedded CLAUSE** (a finite verb, a relative, or
  subject+predicate); a flat NP/maṣdar+PP item rides however long it is (`القضاء على الاحتكار وسيطرة رأس المال`, 6 words) —
  **length is the wrong variable.** Inside a list or layout LINE the LINE is the unit: no cut at و + finite, at a resumptive-PP
  clause, between a lead-in and short items, or at a closing و-clause saying what is done with them. **Reversed for printed
  answer rows by E1.**
- **V12. Backward-facing tails ride on the unit before them:** وهذا / وذلك + a summary; من بينها + NP partitive; a lead-in and
  the QUESTION it introduces; an interrupted trailing fragment. But **وهي / وهو + a bare predicate nominal** commenting on what
  was just named is its OWN unit (`اسمها شهرزاد ‖ وهي مقطوعته الأكثر شهرة`), while **وهي + التي…** continuing the same
  predication JOINS.
- **V13. Utterance-level units — REWRITTEN after batch 8B.** **A SPEECH FRAME CARRIES ITS WHOLE QUOTED TURN as one unit** up to
  ~18 words (`قال همام أسعد الله الصباح أين زاهر يا مدام ‖`, `فسألها قائلا وأنت يا سيدة نعم أنت يا سيدة في هذه المرة لأية قرابة
  ترشحين نفسك ‖` — three sentences, one unit). Break a longer turn at a genuinely new question. Only when the FRAME itself is
  long and elaborated does the quote stand alone (`…بكلمات لا مقدمة لها ولا سابقة لتفسيرها ‖ كم لك من وجوه يا سارة ‖`). A
  stripped one-word question (`لماذا`) and a presentative fragment (`وإليك الجواب`) stand alone. **A vocative never stands
  alone: cut before it, never after it** (`أخطأت يا صديقتي إن فخر المرأة جمالها ‖` is ONE unit). A comment's ADDRESSEE line
  rides with its first sentence; invocations (`علم`) do not; a closing `شكرا` rides on its last sentence.
- **V14. A SPEAKER LABEL IS NOT A SPEECH FRAME — IT IS A LAYOUT LINE AND ITS OWN UNIT (M9).** In an embedded script or dialogue,
  every bare name line (`سارة ‖`) is a unit: 20 misses in one document from riding them into their turns. And when a turn
  ENUMERATES names, each name is a cell (`سارة ‖ دعوت سارة و ‖ سارة ‖ سارة ‖`).

### exercise (worksheet, reader, quiz) and exam papers — family B · 2.5–6 w/unit (exam MCQ 4–4.5)
My worst topic (F1 46 twice) became ten documents at 99-100 by one law. An `exercise` document is almost always an MCQ bank of
**20 items** — and a bank can carry any label (one arrived as `expository`; the grid law still scored 99.0).
- **E1. EVERY PRINTED CELL IS A UNIT.** One unit per item STEM, one per ANSWER OPTION — each `صح`, each `خطأ`, each word of a
  sorting bank, each table cell, each matching entry — however short (`الشورى`, `tRNA`, a bare numeral) and however repetitive.
  This **reverses V11** for this register: printed rows beat item grammar, and in a quiz the page always prints N rows.
- **E2. The stem cuts from its options even when it breaks off mid-syntax** — on a proclitic, a preposition, a construct head, a
  verb+preposition awaiting its object (`يزداد`, `يؤدي إلى`), an exceptive, an apodosis marker or a copula. **Every** dangling
  lead-in cuts. Zero errors in sixteen docs.
- **E3. The STEM CELL IS INDIVISIBLE.** A quoted ayah/hadith with its tag, an embedded imperative or vocative (outranks V13),
  honorifics, a 51-word scenario, stacked relatives, و + finite, garbled OCR — all ride inside one stem.
- **E4. THE OPTION EDGE IS ASYNDETIC — a cell NEVER begins with و.** Every و in an option run is cell-internal coordination,
  including two coordinated maṣdars with PP tails in a 12-word option. Run this check before every option-run cut.
- **E5. Stem material may PRECEDE the interrogative but never FOLLOWS a complete one.** A fronted circumstantial or
  conditional clause rides into the stem it precedes; once the question frame is answerable, everything after it is options.
- **E6. READ THE WHOLE OPTION SET BEFORE PLACING THE STEM'S END** — an MCQ item is a paradigm, so the options are the evidence
  for the stem, not the reverse. If N−1 options are bare single words of one category, the Nth is too: push the surplus word
  into the stem. Never extend the stem past a bare subordinator (عندما، إذا، لأن) to give the option a subject. A generic
  construct head (بلاد، مدينة، سنة) before proper nouns stays with it.
- **E7. Each QUESTION is its own unit even when و-coordinated to the one before it** (`… أسماء التلاميذ ‖ و ما فائدتها ‖`). This
  is a stem edge, not an option edge — E4 does not reach it.
- **E8. Labels and rubrics.** A numbered lesson/section label is its own unit, separate from its title (`الدرس الأول ‖ مدرستي
  ‖`) — the opposite of the legal division-label law. A rubric verb and its qualifier are two lines (`أنشد ‖ بأداء معبر ‖`);
  rubric headings (أستوعب، أتذوق، التعليل) each get a unit; a quotation lead-in DOES cut (`قال الشاعر ‖`); a field LABEL over a
  multi-line block is its own unit (`حياته ‖`), but a label whose value sits on the same line rides with it.
- **E9. Inside a single rubric or instruction line nothing cuts** (و + finite, ثم, coordinated options, a chain of pledge verbs)
  — but in the **PROSE ISLANDS** of a worksheet (a reading paragraph, an answer set, a bio card) the ordinary clause law runs
  again.
- **E10. In a worksheet DIALOGUE the unit is the SPEAKER TURN**, the exact opposite of `conversational`; where the reader is set
  as short 3–6 word lines the turn splits at each phrase. **A poem quoted inside a worksheet splits BELOW the hemistich**, at
  each internal complete predication — poetry's inviolability does not travel.
- **E11. Run the arithmetic check:** total = 20 stems + Σ options; the units must partition ALL the words with the last option
  ending on the last word. The one-unit-per-item reading is excluded by the band alone — that misreading cost 94 errors once.

### hadith — **FAMILY C, not A** (one graded document, F1 66.7)
- **H1. Hadith is NOT family A.** Family A works for quran/bible/poetry because those sources carry a canonical division mark and
  NO ordinary punctuation. A printed hadith edition punctuates freely INSIDE each hadith, so the unit is the edition's sentence
  and the connective table is LIVE. Confirmed: the key cut a 54-word single hadith into four units.
- **H2.** The **isnad chain rides with the FIRST sentence of the matn** (`قال مالك أخبرني… أنه سمع رسول الله ﷺ يقول إذا أسلم
  العبد… كان زلفها ‖`) — V13's frame rule and G3's no-cut-before-a-quotation both hold. Then ordinary law: و+finite cuts once the
  period has run long, and a colon paradigm inside the matn splits into cells (G3).

### quran — family A · 5/5 documents at F1 100.0, 302 boundaries, zero errors
- **Q1. THE AYAH IS THE SENTENCE** — the verse marker is the only punctuation scripture carries, so the only mark this corpus
  could have stripped. Below the ayah the connective table is inoperative BOTH ways: it refuses cuts my rules demand (و + finite
  ~40 times inside verses; a 43-word 2:164 whole across asyndetic topic jumps; فـ، ثم، imperative restarts) and takes cuts they
  forbid (before a relative on the preceding noun, a purpose-lām, a bare ḥāl, mid-construct). Checks: every boundary carries the
  fāsila; count = the sura's division; all words used. **Never use density** — 6.4–17.0 w/unit across five perfect documents.

### bible (Van Dyck) — family A · 5/5 documents where key cuts = verse count exactly · 11–14.5 w/unit
- **B1. THE UNIT IS THE VERSE, EXACTLY THE VERSE.** Gen 4:25–5:32 = 34; Matt 19:27–20:34 = 38; Mark 2:23–3:35 = 41; Luke
  12:54–13:35 = 41; Luke 16:1–17:19 = 50 — key counts, to the digit.
- **B2. NOTHING inside a verse cuts** — not a change of SPEAKER (`قالوا له … قال لهم` in one verse joins), not a QUESTION, not a
  bare VOCATIVE, not a bare IMPERATIVE or DENIAL, not بل, not و / فـ + finite, not a fronted temporal, and above all **not a
  full stop the edition shows**. **And EVERY verse edge cuts through the syntax**, including between a speech frame and its
  quotation (`فقال لهم ‖ اجتهدوا`).
- **B3. Do NOT model the boundary as "the printed sentence of the edition"** — that cost 42 of my 43 bible errors. Find the
  edges by passage identity, verse onset (و / فـ + a perfect verb, or a re-stated proper name), and the verse COUNT as the check.

### poetry — family A · F1 100.0, 133 boundaries, zero errors
- **P1. THE PRINTED VERSE LINE IS THE SENTENCE; each poem's title is one unit. WHICH line depends on the metre.**
  **LONG-METRE classical diwan verse: the بيت (صدر + عجز, closing on the rhyme) is the unit** — F1 100.0, 133 boundaries.
  **مجزوء / SHORT-LINE verse (children's poems, songs, hymns): each شطر is its own printed line and its own unit** —
  `ما أجمل الغيوم ‖ تسير أو تحوم ‖`, `بعيدها السحاب ‖ قريبها الرباب ‖`, `ويبدأ الرذاذ ‖ ثم يأتي المطر ‖`.
  **Discriminator: if a candidate بيت is under ~8 words, the printed line is the HEMISTICH — cut at the caesura.**
  Count lines, never density. Syntax neither creates nor cancels a boundary: enjambment runs freely across the caesura and the
  line end, and subordinators, coordinated NPs, ثم and unfinished conditionals straddling a line edge are cut anyway (M2).
- **P2. VERSE QUOTED INSIDE ANY OTHER LABEL SPLITS AT EVERY HEMISTICH, AND BELOW IT AT EVERY COMPLETE PREDICATION.** 14 misses
  on one anthology page from applying P1 there: `أبلغ سليمان أني لست في سعة ‖ وفي غنى غير أني لست ذا مال ‖ يسخو بنفسي أني لا أرى
  أحدا ‖ يموت هزلا…‖`, and finer still: `ليس البلاغة معنى ‖ فيه الكلام يطول ‖ بل صوغ معنى كثير ‖ يحويه لفط قليل ‖`.
  This is E10's law generalised: poetry's inviolability does not travel out of its own label. **بل cuts at such a hemistich
  edge — the only place بل ever cuts.**
- **P3. A CHANT or REFRAIN splits into its PRINTED LINES — AND NEVER BELOW THEM. One unit per repeat is WRONG.**
  A refrain that fills one line is ONE unit: `بات مان ×6 ‖ الحارس الذي لا يهادن الشر ‖` (atomising it cost 21
  precision points, my worst document of four batches). A chant only splits where it has an obvious line PERIOD:
  `لا ×8 ‖ لا ×8 ‖ لا ×8 ‖` and `داي داي داي ‖ داي الشجاع ‖`. **If the run's length is not an obvious multiple of
  some line, treat the whole run as ONE line.** M1's test both ways: too coarse cost 4 recall points, too fine cost 21.

### conversational (comic strip, balloons) — family C · ≈4 w/unit
- **C1. The unit is the UTTERANCE.** Every change of speaker is a boundary, and inside one balloon EVERY complete sentence is
  its own unit — statement, question, imperative, exclamation, bare echo, one-word reaction, laughter (`ها ها`). **Contrast
  E10:** a balloon is not a reader-line. Nothing else cuts: PP conjuncts sharing an elided head and all subordinate material
  stay inside. Each header element and the caption box get their own unit, plus every bare SPEAKER LABEL (V14); a masthead's
  SERIES name and EPISODE title share one line while the credits below are one unit each. **A comic strip labelled `narrative`
  still obeys C1** — the tell is a رسوم credit and nothing but alternating short turns (F1 86.5 at 4.1 w/unit).

### legal (constitution, statute) — family C/B hybrid · the unit is the PROVISION / LINE · F1 97.4
- **G1. Inside one provision the annotators do NOT cut at every finite clause.** و + finite, and even an asyndetic new finite
  predication, stay in the article; a closing formula (`والله الموفق`) rides on the last provision. This is where M1 was first
  found: the unit is the line, not the clause. The boundary is the asyndetic junction between provision-units; heading and
  article alternate as separate units.
- **G2. A division label and its topic title are ONE unit** — never cut between `الباب / الفصل + ordinal` and its title; **but a
  NUMBERED ARTICLE label is its own unit** (`‖ مادة 282 ‖`). Reversed for worksheets by E8. A marginal article title restating
  the article's opening words: cut between title and body, not inside it.
- **G3. A stripped COLON introducing an enumeration is a full boundary** when the lead-in is a dangling PREPOSITION (`الحق في
  ‖`) or a full predication awaiting its list (`يحظر عليها ‖`) — NOT after an identifying copular هي / وهي in prose, and NOT
  before a QUOTATION. **A lead-in shares the fate of its items**: items ride together → the lead-in rides with them; items split
  → the lead-in is its own unit. **And a colon PARADIGM (X gets a, Y gets b) always splits, lead-in included** — the hadith
  `وكان بعد ذلك القصاص ‖ الحسنة بعشر أمثالها ‖ والسيئة بمثلها ‖` is four units, not two.
- **G4.** كما + a finite verb DOES open a new unit (but see X5's comparative/additive split). A fronted adverbial belongs to the
  FOLLOWING unit — but it only says WHERE a boundary goes, it does not create one.

## PA APPENDIX — punctuated-track law (graded)

Written after **batch A** of the Pnx-PA practice (5 expository documents, 307 gold boundaries). Headline result:
**precision 100.0 on all five documents, recall 68.5 / 96.4 / 90.0 / 47.4 / 36.5.** Every printed mark I cut at was
right; every one of my 106 errors was a MISS. **So on this track F11 inverts: when torn at a printed mark, CUT.
Recall, not precision, is my live failure here.** Everything in PARTS 1-3 above still holds — this appendix only adds
what the marks do.

### PA1. THE MARK TIERS — what the annotators do at each printed sign
- **`.` `؟` `!` ALWAYS end the sentence, and the boundary sits ON the mark.** 122 sites, zero false cuts. This holds
  inside a long quoted turn too (…بهذه الطريقة ؟ ‖ المباراة كانت رائعة . ‖) — V13's "the frame carries its whole turn"
  is de-punctuated law and does NOT survive into a punctuated quote.
- **A DOUBLED `. .` is a journalistic PAUSE, not a sentence end — both dots ride** (6/6: `ال 14 . . بدأت`,
  `والبحرين . . إلى جانب`, `منتخب الإمارات . . وكأس أمم آسيا`, `إنجازات كبيرة . . كان أهمها`,
  `( الوصيف ) . . محققا`, `من اللاعبين . . والجمهور`). **A TRIPLED `. . .` IS an ellipsis that ends the sentence, and
  the boundary goes on the LAST dot** — my miss `…والآداب والفلسفة . . [[.]] ‖ كل شيء قد ينال حظا…`.
- **An abbreviation dot is not a sentence end**: `يقول ر . كابوشنسكي أن…` rides (I suppressed it and was right).
- **A CLOSING quote or paren after the mark takes the boundary**: `…وبصره الذي يبصر به . " ‖ <NL>` — the sentence ends
  on the `"`, not on the `.`; otherwise the closer is stranded as a wordless unit.
- **Track law, free of charge: every token before an `<NL>` and the last token of the document carry a boundary —
  even when that token is a bare comma** (`…فهو لا محالة قادر عليه وعالم به , ‖ <NL> ألا يعلم من خلق…`).

### PA2. `؛` CUTS BEFORE A NEW INDEPENDENT CLAUSE AND RIDES BEFORE A SUBORDINATE ONE (8 cuts / 6 rides)
**CUTS:** `؛ فإنهم`, `؛ عندئذ`, `؛ حتى الأعمال`, `؛ فعلم` (x2), `؛ فطلب`, `؛ أحدهما`, and **`؛ إذ` (3/3)**.
**RIDES:** `؛ لأن` (3/3), `؛ ولهذا`, `؛ من مختلف نواحي` (a bare PP), `؛ تبين له` (the apodosis of a ولما-clause).
Note the split my own doctrine predicted: **X8's إذ cuts, M7's لأن joins** — the mark does not override the connective,
it only makes the site visible. My batch-A law "؛ is a mid-sentence mark, ride all of them" was wrong 8 times.

### PA3. THE COMMA `,` / `،` — cuts only when a NEW INDEPENDENT FINITE CLAUSE opens, and only as far as the BAND allows
This is where all my remaining misses lived (~75 of 106). The comma is promoted to a sentence end when the next
element opens an independent clause: **ف + anything** (فلا يمكن، فليست، فرأى، فتقهقر، فإذن، فاللاعب، فإننا، فإذا أخطأ),
**و + a finite verb** (واستمرت، وشاركت، وتمكن، واستطاع، وأقيمت، وشهدت، وبلغت، ووقع، وصعد، واحتل، وعلم، ورأى، وحدثت),
**و + a new nominal subject** (والطين، والذي، وهي تتمثل، وقد شابهت، **وذلك + a CAUSE** — X6 exactly), **a bare asyndetic
finite verb** (`…قبل بداية البطولة , ‖ تتأهل المنتخبات… , ‖ يقسم المنتخبات… , ‖ يتأهل الأفضل…`), **ثم إن**,
**غير أن / إلا أن**, and **additive كما + finite** (`…في المركز الرابع , ‖ كما وصل منتخب الأردن…` — X5 confirmed).
**It NEVER cuts before:** an أن/وأن complement, **لأن**, a relative (الذي/التي/مما), a purpose lām (ليكون، ليحصل),
**بل** (3/3), **لكن/ولكن (4/4 — X3 confirmed)**, حيث, على حين / في حين, خاصة أن, إلا أنه, عندما / بعدما / مثلما,
إما…وإما, an enumeration item, an apposition, a ḥāl, or **a fronted adverbial that belongs to the NEXT clause**
(`. وفي عام 1980 , أقيمت البطولة…` is ONE unit — F5 again: the comma after a fronted PP is never the boundary).

### PA4. THE BAND IS STILL THE GATE — and it decides HOW MANY commas get promoted (M8 for this track)
Measured gold densities, words per unit, batch A: **newspaper/magazine LAYOUT PAGE 15.7 · modern encyclopedia
(Wikipedia) 18.6 · modern literary ESSAY (first-person argument) 23.8 · CLASSICAL / turath prose 14.1 and 16.2.**
**NEW SUB-REGISTER: classical pre-modern Arabic prose (Ibn Tufayl and the like) segments at 14-16, NOT at the
18-24 of modern authored prose** — it was the two turath documents that wrecked me (F1 53.5 and 64.3).
**Procedure, every PA document:** (1) lay the hard marks of PA1-PA2; (2) count words per unit; (3) if that is above the
sub-register band, walk the commas in order and promote every one that opens an independent clause, stopping when the
band is reached; (4) if it is inside the band, promote nothing (`doc_201b37ae8a22`: hard 19.3, gold 18.6 — two
promotions in the two longest units only; `doc_de12d5da4854`: hard 26.4, gold 23.8 — the promotions were its ؛ and its
ellipsis, and **not one of its 53 commas**). A dense-comma journalistic page and a turath page both need ~40% of their
commas promoted; an essay needs none. This scaffold scores 87.4 mean F1 on batch A against 78.4 for marks alone.

### PA5. THE COLON `:` — G3 survives verbatim, and the label test decides
**CUTS:** a starred / numbered run-in HEADING whose following clause has its own subject (`* أولا اللاعب : ‖ أول من
يتحمل المسئولية…`, `* ثانيا حكم المباراة : ‖ وعليه أن يراعي…`, `* خامسا لجنة التنظيم : ‖ منظمو البطولات…`); a complete
predication awaiting list items that are themselves sentences (`…مترددين ومتخالفين : ‖ ففريق منا يدعو…`,
`…في الحياة الجسمانية : ‖ من المعلوم أن…`); and a lead-in whose items are printed on following lines
(`…ثلاث مرات : ‖ <NL> إيران أعوام 1968…`, `…بغير زمان : ‖ <NL> إنما أمره…`).
**RIDES:** a byline label (`إعداد : خالد فؤاد`); an appositive colon (`وبتعبير أدق : الموازنة…`,
`يعم جميع الأجسام : حيها وجمادها`); an identifying هي/وهي whose list rides inside one sentence
(`وهي : إندونيسيا , وماليزيا , وفيتنام , وتايلاند .`); **a SPEECH FRAME** (`قال الحكم : أتمنى…`, `وقال معلقا : لا أفهم…`,

### PA12. FINAL SHAPE OF THE PA PROCEDURE
1. **Family first (M1/F6).** Family B (grid) and family A: `<NL>` has already printed the cells — take them, add only

2. **Lay the hard marks**: `.` `؟` `!` each end a sentence, boundary ON the mark; a RUN of marks takes one boundary on
   its last token; an abbreviation dot, a decimal point, a thousands comma and a mark inside an embedded quotation are
   not sentence ends; a closing quote takes the boundary only when `<NL>` follows; every token before `<NL>` and the

3. **Judge every `. .` / `. . .` by PA11a**, not by a blanket rule — this alone was 22 boundaries in one document.
4. **Words ÷ units, against the PA10 band.** In band → stop. Above it → promote `؛` then `،` then unmarked clause
