# Research prompt v3: beating a fully-diagnosed low-resource Arabic segmentation ceiling

You are a skeptical NLP researcher. Below: a completely diagnosed problem (every number independently verified), the full graveyard of what failed, the newly-discovered meta-obstacle ("ensemble absorption"), and two idea sketches to develop. **We need methods that (a) fix SYSTEMATIC errors at the source or (b) produce voters with DIFFERENT error patterns — because same-direction solo improvements provably get absorbed by our ensemble.** Cite only real papers (venue+year, measured effect sizes). Do not re-suggest anything in the graveyard.

## 1. Task and constraint
AraSeg 2026 closed track: **174 labeled Arabic documents** (222 dev / 262 test), binary "sentence boundary after token i", **per-document macro-F1**. No external data, no generated text, **no dev-set training (explicit organizer ruling)**. Pretrained encoders allowed. Hardest track "NoPnx-NP": all punctuation and paragraph marks removed. Same 174 docs exist in 4 aligned views (punctuation/paragraphs on/off). Current: single encoder ≈83.5, 6-encoder ensemble + semi-Markov decode ≈85.0 dev / 84.97 test. ~4th place; top-3 beat us on identical data. Seed-noise floor ±0.45.

## 2. The verified diagnosis (all numbers independently reproduced)
1. **Memorization anchor:** train F1 **99.02** vs dev **83.01** — a **+16-point** generalization gap (26 errors in 15,363 train decisions). The model near-memorized its 174 docs and carries that world's confidence into new text.
2. **Confidently wrong, fire-side only:** 97.9% of outputs at extremes (P>0.9 or <0.1); median stated probability on a WRONG cut = **0.992**. A "99.5%-sure CUT" is right only **84.1%** of the time (+15.3-pt gap); a confident NO-CUT is honest (+0.9). In cloze/exercise text, **44% of ultra-confident cuts (P≥0.99) are wrong**.
3. **Errors are SYSTEMATIC, not seed lottery:** **79.5% of one seed's false cuts are shared by all 3 seeds.** A better recipe can fix them in principle; seed-averaging cannot.
4. **The damage is a broad shallow bleed, not a doc tail:** worst 15 of 222 docs carry only **14.8%** of the missing score; 74% lives in ordinary mid-range documents. No per-document rescue works.
5. **Bidirectional genre failure with a ~9-token length prior** (gold 9.88 tokens/sentence → predicted 9.04): OVER-cuts cloze (ratio 1.387; the blank-token فراغ alone sits before 99 false cuts; fixing 4 cloze docs = +0.54 macro), legal lists (1.175), long sentences, after bare numbers (2.5×), document ends (2×); UNDER-cuts (P≈0.000) genealogy chains (بن X بن Y — one seed under-cuts the genealogy doc 0.889 missing 17/27 boundaries while its sibling seed OVER-cuts the same doc 1.852: **the model has no learned convention at all there**), hadith isnad, Quranic verse openings, connective-initial sentences (و/ثم/كما), and short sentences (1–6 tokens lose their endings at 13–14%).
6. **Coverage wall:** 66–74% of confident errors sit on words/word-pairs absent from training; ~95% of ALL dev bigrams are unseen — so unfamiliarity signals CANNOT separate wrong cuts from true boundaries (every OOV/perplexity gate: AUC ≈ 0.5, measured).
7. **Gold is fine:** ≤3.5% of confident errors are plausibly annotation error.

## 3. THE META-OBSTACLE: ensemble absorption (this is what kills "good" ideas here)
Measured pattern: solo-encoder gains die at the ensemble/test level because the 6 voters' errors are correlated (0.74 mean off-diagonal):
- Multi-view/union training (train on stripped + punctuated views jointly): solo **+0.9** (all seeds, real!) → pool +0.46 → **test: exact tie**.
- smooth+FGM: solo +0.68 → test −0.06. Verdict of record: "ensemble absorbs per-voter gains."
- R-Drop: solo +0.37 (only survivor of a 15-method sweep) → pool fate unknown, same risk.
All failed diversity-adders (CAMeLBERT, SaT, mmBERT, soups, aug voters) were the SAME decision procedure (token classifiers). **Ask #1 below follows from this.**

## 4. What we need from you (ranked)
1. **Voters with structurally DIFFERENT error patterns.** We are testing an end-to-end Filtered Semi-Markov CRF (segment-level scoring with learned duration potentials) and MRT (minimum-risk training on the metric). What else produces *decorrelated* errors for sequence labeling — pointer/boundary-generation decoders, energy-based/global renormalized models, span-enumeration classifiers, retrieval-augmented tagging over the train set, latent segmentation models? Evidence for ensemble gains from procedure-diversity (not model-diversity) specifically?
2. **Source-level fixes for the SHARED systematic errors** (the 79.5%): especially (a) the cloze/exercise shredding (+0.54 sitting in 4 docs — genre-targeted decode rules? test-time structure detection?), (b) the chain-genre under-cutting where no convention was learned (genealogy/isnad/verse — can a length/duration prior conditioned on local repetition structure fix chains?), (c) connective-initial missed boundaries.
3. **Develop this idea sketch (from the team): boundary-pair contrastive learning.** Raw version: for the pair of spans around a candidate position, push their representations APART if a boundary separates them (label 1) and PULL TOGETHER if not (label 0), anchored at label positions/connectives. Known risks measured here: 4 contrastive cousins all nulled (token-SupCon, paragraph-contrastive, coherence-shuffle head, SimCSE), and adjacent same-topic sentences are semantically close — push-apart is weakest exactly on the hard chain boundaries. Sharpened version we want assessed: contrast **true-boundary contexts vs the model's own confident-false-cut contexts** (we have 2,236 mined hard negatives) with a margin — i.e., aim the contrast at the measured failure (false cuts) instead of at semantics. Is there literature on hard-negative contrastive training from a model's own high-confidence errors for sequence labeling? Does it survive the "absorbed calibration slide" trap (5 anti-overconfidence losses already nulled: fire-side penalty, entropy penalty, label smoothing, soft-F1, focal-style)?
4. **Anything that breaks a +16-point memorization gap at n=174** beyond what's tried/running (running now: SAM/class-SAM, Group-DRO with length pseudo-groups, MRT, semi-CRF). Meta-learning episodes over pseudo-genres? Retrieval/nearest-neighbor inference (kNN over train token representations — closed-legal!)? Bayesian/ensemble-in-one-net approaches with genuinely different inductive biases?

## 5. Graveyard — do NOT re-suggest (all measured, 3 seeds, ±0.45 floor)
Preposition/connective-targeted consistency (counterfactual, Mean-Teacher, CVT): null. Char-level augmentation: worsened over-firing. SUB2 span-swap: null. Recombination: −1.56. TAPT: −0.61. DAGA: −3.5. Token SupCon / SimCSE / paragraph-contrastive / coherence-shuffle: null. Fire-side confidence penalty (−0.07), entropy penalty, label smoothing (−0.10), soft-F1: null. OOV/perplexity abstention: AUC≈0.5, dead. Multi-stride/center-weighted decode: +0.117 sub-floor. Larger encoders (AraBERT-large, XLM-R-large, mmBERT, CAMeLBERT): all < base. Recursive re-reading: killed by its battery. Union/multi-view 2-view training: +0.9 solo, absorbed, test tie. Punctuation-dropout (2 variants): lost to union. Cross-view privileged distillation (PA-teacher→NoPnx-student): built, gated behind a teacher-sharpness diagnostic, deprioritized after the absorption finding. Genre-conditional decode thresholds: null (too few genre docs). Adding voters (SaT, CAMeLBERT, soups, w240): pool saturated at 6.
