## 1. HEADLINE

**Transfer next: DBR — Divergence-Based Regularization for Debiasing NLU Models (Li et al., arXiv:2502.18353, Feb 2025; published in ACM SIGKDD Explorations).**

- **Repo:** none official — reimplement from the paper (~60 lines). Integrated Gradients is one `captum` call or a hand-rolled Riemann sum on our own embeddings; both are in-tree already (torch autograd).
- **Why it, and not the crowd of consistency losses:** DBR is the *only* legal method in the whole hunt that targets our #1 failure mode **mechanistically**. It uses Integrated Gradients on the model's own predictions to find the surface tokens/bigrams the boundary head is over-relying on, soft-masks exactly those, and adds `λ·JSD(p_masked ‖ p_orig)`. That is a direct penalty on "I decided this boundary because of one seen bigram" — which is 88–89% of our error mass. SimCSE/SupCon/Dice/soft-F1 do **not** address shortcut reliance at all; R-Drop/Cutoff attack it only diffusely (random/structured masking, not *attribution-targeted* masking). Reported OOD gains are large and consistent (MNLI→HANS +6.2, FEVER→Sym1 +4.1, QQP→PAWS +8.3) with little in-domain loss, and OOD-under-distribution-shift is precisely the unseen-bigram regime.
- **Honest expected NoPnx F1 vs the ±0.45 floor:** I expect **+0.6 to +1.2 F1 per single model on NoPnx-NP/PA**, i.e. it should clear the ±0.45 seed floor on a single voter — *but* the paper's headline deltas are on cross-dataset NLI/paraphrase, not Arabic boundary tagging, and our DP ensemble will absorb part of any per-voter gain. After ensemble+DP I'd forecast **+0.3 to +0.7 F1 at the board level**, which is right at the edge of the floor. This is the one method where the *mechanism* matches the *diagnosis*, so it has the highest expected value even though it needs reimplementation. Gate it on multi-seed (see §3).

Runner-up for "safest cheap win": **R-Drop** (NeurIPS 2021, `github.com/dropreg/R-Drop`, official HF token-classification code) — near-free, symmetric per-token KL, but higher absorption risk because it is a single smooth regularizer the ensemble is good at eating.

## 3. TRANSFER PLANS — TOP 3

### P0-a — DBR (attribution-guided shortcut suppression)

- **Clone/base:** no repo. Reimplement `Loss = CE(orig) + λ·JSD(p_masked ‖ p_orig)`. Integrated Gradients over input embeddings against the boundary logits (20–50 Riemann steps, baseline = zero-embedding), take top-k attributed content tokens per window, soft-mask them (scale embedding by `(1−m)`), second forward pass, JSD on the per-token boundary posteriors.
- **File to touch:** `arabic-sentence-segmentation/src/train_encoder.py` **only**. New flags `--dbr-weight` (λ, default 0.0 = bit-identical baseline), `--dbr-topk`, `--dbr-ig-steps`. Add the term inside `WeightedTrainer.compute_loss` (L201) exactly where `--supcon-weight` composes (L265–281) — same additive, flag-gated pattern. The second forward reuses the FGM precedent in `training_step` (L283) for how to do a re-forward cleanly. **No new dependency** (autograd IG hand-rolled; do not add captum).
- **Mask legality:** masking *input context* never moves a "boundary-after-token-i" label → label-preserving, category (c). Compute on the 174 docs only.
- **A/B protocol vs ±0.45 floor:** train NoPnx-NP and NoPnx-PA at λ∈{0.5, 1.0} × **5 seeds** each, plus 5-seed λ=0 control from the same code path. Report per-doc macro-F1 mean ± std. **Adopt only if mean(DBR) − mean(control) > 0.45 AND the 5-seed min beats the control mean** (clears the floor, not just the mean). Then re-run the single winning config through the 6-model+DP ensemble to measure post-absorption board delta. Log to a new dated `runs/` dir (append-only).

### P0-b — Model Stock (seed averaging, attacks the ±0.45 floor itself)

- **Clone:** `github.com/naver-ai/model-stock`. Use only the closed-form interpolation `t = 2cosθ/(1+cosθ)` between two same-arch fine-tuned seeds, anchored on the pretrained encoder. ~30 lines; no training loop change.
- **File to touch:** **new module** `arabic-sentence-segmentation/src/model_stock.py` (a post-hoc weight merge over checkpoints), consumed by `ensemble_sweep.py` / `weighted_ensemble.py`. Does **not** touch `train_encoder.py` — it operates on saved state-dicts. **No val set** required, which is why it is legal where greedy model-soup is not.
- **Mechanism fit:** merge 2–3 seeds *within each* of the 6 encoders → each voter becomes flatter/lower-variance *before* the DP sees it. This is the correct response to a seed floor: don't pick a lucky seed, average the shell.
- **A/B protocol:** for one encoder (AraBERTv02) train 3 seeds on NoPnx-NP; compare (i) best-single-seed voter, (ii) 3-seed Model-Stock merged voter — each fed through the full DP ensemble. Metric: board macro-F1 **and** its seed-to-seed std. **Adopt if merged-voter std drops and mean is ≥ single-seed mean − 0.1.** The win here is variance reduction; it should shrink the ±0.45 floor mechanically. Cheap enough to run same-day.

### P1 — R-Drop (symmetric per-token output KL)

- **Clone:** `github.com/dropreg/R-Drop` — has ready HF transformer NLU code; port the token-classification KL (mask -100 and `[PAR]` positions, per-token symmetric KL, average over content tokens).
- **File to touch:** `train_encoder.py`, `WeightedTrainer.compute_loss` (L201). Two forward passes with independent dropout (the Trainer already runs dropout in train mode; second pass is one extra forward). New flag `--rdrop-weight` (α, default 0.0). Add `α·[KL(p1‖p2)+KL(p2‖p1)]/2` per token, masked to content positions — same additive slot as SupCon.
- **Why P1 not P0:** it is the *cheapest* and strictly upgrades our shelved SimCSE consistency, but it is a single smooth signal — exactly what the 6-model+DP has shown it can absorb. Treat a null result as a **fast, informative negative** (confirms absorption), not a surprise.
- **A/B protocol:** 5 seeds × {α=0.5, α=1.0} vs 5-seed α=0 control on both NoPnx tracks. Same "> 0.45 mean AND min-beats-control-mean" gate. Because it also tightens per-token posteriors, additionally check whether it improves DP-potential calibration (lower ECE on the boundary head) even if raw F1 is flat — a calibration win can still help the DP decode.

**Sequencing:** run **Model Stock (P0-b) first** — it is a same-day, no-retrain variance win and it *cleans the measurement floor* that every other A/B is fighting. Then **DBR (P0-a)** as the one mechanism-matched bet. Then **R-Drop (P1)** as the cheap A/B. All three stack (weight-merge ∘ shortcut-loss ∘ KL-loss are orthogonal) and every default reproduces yesterday's behavior (flags default to 0/off).

## 5. BLUNT BOTTOM LINE

**Yes — one method genuinely beats what we have, and it is DBR (arXiv 2502.18353, SIGKDD Explorations 2025).** It is the single item in the entire hunt whose *mechanism* (attribution-targeted masking of over-relied-on tokens + JSD consistency) matches our *diagnosis* (88–89% of errors are unseen-bigram shortcut reliance). Everything else we already have — Dice, soft-F1, SupCon, SimCSE, FGM adversarial — either reweights the pointwise loss or adds diffuse noise; **none of them punish shortcut reliance directly.** DBR does. That is a real, un-tried lever, not a fourth flavor of the same consistency term.

**But be sober about the ceiling.** We are #1 on all four boards, NoPnx has only ~13–15 F1 of headroom, the errors live in genuinely unseen bigrams the 174 docs simply do not contain, and our 6-model+DP ensemble demonstrably absorbs single weak signals. The honest forecast is **+0.3 to +0.7 board-level F1 from DBR after ensemble** — a real gain that clears the ±0.45 seed floor on a single voter but sits *at* the floor after absorption. Model Stock's job is to *lower that floor* so the DBR signal becomes measurable; that is why it runs first.

**We are close to the closed-track ceiling, not at it.** The residual headroom is real but thin, and it is bounded by information the 174 docs don't contain — no loss function manufactures unseen bigrams from nothing. The realistic ceiling of this whole shortlist, stacked, is roughly **+0.5 to +1.5 F1 on NoPnx**, with DBR + Model Stock carrying most of it. Anything promising more than that on a strict 174-doc closed track is either illegal or overfitting the seed noise. Run Model Stock today, DBR this week, R-Drop as the cheap tie-breaker; hold the rest as benched fallbacks.
